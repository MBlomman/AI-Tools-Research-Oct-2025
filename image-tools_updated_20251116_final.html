Critical Legal Update

⚠️ EU AI Act Compliance Deadline: August 2, 2026

All AI-generated content must be transparently watermarked and labeled as synthetic.

Current Compliant Solutions:

  • ✅ Google SynthID: Invisible, robust watermarking embedded in image generation
  • ✅ Adobe Content Credentials: C2PA standard with metadata and provenance tracking

Recommended Action for Swedish Agencies:

  • Begin planning implementation of watermarking workflows now
  • Prioritize tools with built-in compliance (Google, Adobe)
  • Audit current AI content generation processes
