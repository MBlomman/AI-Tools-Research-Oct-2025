⚖️ Legal & Compliance Framework

Critical Legal Deadlines

  • August 2, 2025: EU AI Act core GPAI (General Purpose AI) obligations became enforceable
  • August 2, 2026: Mandatory synthetic content watermarking (machine-readable) for all GPAI outputs
  • Ongoing: GDPR enforcement intensifying across EU - €15M OpenAI fine (March 2025), Grok investigation (April 2025), DeepSeek ban in Italy (January 2025)

Compliance Checklist for Swedish Agencies

Before using any LLM for commercial client work, verify:

  • ✅ Enterprise/Business account (NOT Free/Plus/Pro tiers)
  • ✅ EU data residency enabled and verified
  • ✅ Data Processing Addendum (DPA) signed with provider
  • ✅ Zero training on client data clause in agreement
  • ✅ Zero or minimal data retention (7-30 days maximum)
  • ✅ Encryption at rest and in transit (AES-256, TLS 1.2+)
  • ✅ GDPR compliance documentation provided by vendor
  • ✅ ISO 42001 / SOC 2 certification (or equivalent)
  • ✅ August 2026 watermarking compliance pathway (SynthID, Content Credentials, or alternative)
  • ❌ Avoid models with Chinese data storage or government access
  • ❌ Avoid models under active GDPR investigation without legal clearance
  • ❌ Avoid models without required EU representative
  • ❌ Avoid models with recent GDPR violations or bans

EU AI Act Watermarking Requirements

By August 2, 2026, all synthetic content must include machine-readable watermarks.

Compliant Solutions:

  • Google SynthID: Integrated into Gemini models, detects AI-generated text, images, audio, and video
  • Adobe Content Credentials: Industry standard for content provenance, supports multiple media types
  • C2PA (Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity): Open standard being adopted by multiple providers

Current Status by Provider:

  • ✅ Google (Gemini): SynthID fully implemented and operational
  • ⚠️ OpenAI: No public watermarking implementation announced yet
  • ⚠️ Anthropic: No public watermarking implementation announced yet
  • ⚠️ xAI (Grok): No watermarking information available
  • ❌ Chinese providers: No EU AI Act compliance pathway

Recent GDPR Enforcement Actions

  • March 2025: OpenAI fined €15 million by Italian Garante for GDPR violations related to training data collection
  • April 11, 2025: Irish DPC opens formal investigation into xAI's Grok following 9 GDPR complaints about unauthorized data scraping from X/Twitter
  • January 30, 2025: Italian Garante bans DeepSeek for Chinese data storage, lack of EU representative, and failure to meet GDPR requirements
  • June 27, 2025: Germany requests DeepSeek removal from app stores citing GDPR concerns and data sovereignty issues
