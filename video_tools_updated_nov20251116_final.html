Critical Legal Update

⚠️ EU AI Act Compliance Deadline: August 2, 2026

All AI-generated content must be transparently watermarked and labeled as synthetic.

Current Compliant Solutions:

  • ✅ Google SynthID: Invisible, robust watermarking embedded in video generation

Recommended Action for Swedish Agencies:

  • Begin planning implementation of watermarking workflows now
  • Prioritize tools with built-in compliance (Google Veo)
  • Audit current AI video content generation processes
  • Prepare client communication regarding watermarking

⚠️ Chinese Tools: Legal & Data Sovereignty Risks

Tools like Kling AI, Minimax Hailuo, Seedance, and Wan: While technically impressive, these Chinese tools carry significant legal risks for EU agencies:

  • Non-EU jurisdiction raises GDPR compliance questions
  • No GDPR documentation or unclear data handling
  • Undisclosed training data sources
  • Potential data sovereignty issues under Chinese cybersecurity laws
  • Unclear commercial licensing terms for European markets
  • No clear EU AI Act compliance pathway

Impact for agencies: High legal risk for commercial client work. Use only for personal experimentation and skill development pending comprehensive legal review.
